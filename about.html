Our story

EcoCycle began in 2019 with two trucks and one coastal town. Residents wanted a pickup they could rely on and proof that recyclables were really recycled. We built weighed receipts, certified processing partners and a reward for sorting well.

  • Own crews and certified processing partners
  • Open reporting on every tonne collected
  • Eco-points for every verified kilo
48,000+households served
1,200 twaste recycled
40service areas
62%less landfill per household

Our mission

Keep recyclable material out of landfill by making sorting and pickup effortless for every household.

Our vision

Cities where every household knows exactly where its waste goes and is rewarded for doing the right thing.
