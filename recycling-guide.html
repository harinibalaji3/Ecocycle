Recycling guide

What goes where, in plain terms.

Recyclable

Clean paper, cardboard, PET and HDPE plastic, cans, glass.

Organic

Food scraps, garden waste, tea leaves.

Electronic

Phones, chargers, batteries, small appliances.

Landfill

Used tissues, ceramics, soiled packaging.

Common questions

Are pizza boxes recyclable?

Only the clean part. Tear off greasy sections.

Do bottle caps stay on?

Yes, screw caps back on.
